Common questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What is your commission rate?
We charge a fixed 15% commission on gross rental income for our Listing Management plan, and 25% for Full Management, which covers cleaning coordination, maintenance and 24/7 support. There are no registration fees, no setup costs, and no monthly minimums.
How long is the management contract?
There’s no minimum contract length. We work on a rolling monthly basis, and you can cancel with 30 days’ notice at any time. We’re confident in the results, so we don’t rely on lock-ins to keep clients.
Who is liable if a guest causes damage?
All properties we manage are covered by Airbnb’s AirCover protection (up to $3 million USD). We also conduct a full property inventory check before every booking and collect a security deposit from every guest. Any damage is documented, photographed, and pursued through the platform.
How does Law No. 7464 affect my property?
Law No. 7464, which came into effect in 2024, requires all short-term rental operators in Turkey to obtain a permit from the Ministry of Culture and Tourism. OB Stays handles the entire permit application process for every property we manage, from the paperwork to the submission to ongoing compliance monitoring, at no additional cost.
How are taxes and legal processes handled?
We work with licensed accountants and legal advisors to ensure your rental income is declared correctly under Turkish law. We’ll guide you through VAT obligations, income tax reporting, and platform-specific tax documentation. You won’t be left to figure this out alone.
When and how do I receive my payments?
Rental income is transferred directly to your bank account on a fixed monthly schedule, typically within the first 5 business days of the following month. You’ll receive a full statement showing all reservations, revenue, and any applicable deductions.
