About

Beth Darroch is a freelance fashion and culture journalist based in London.

She has studied fashion at University of the Arts London since 2021, and writes on fashion, music, nightlife and the arts. Her work has appeared in to Be Magazine and Shift London, and in 10 Magazine, where she was an editorial intern in 2024.

She covers runway trends and beauty launches alongside longer interviews with musicians and designers, and reports on the cost of breaking into fashion for the people trying to do it.
